Background

Robert Mikulik is a neurologist with a focus on vascular neurology and especially on strokes. He is Head of the Complex Cerebrovascular Center at St. Anne's University hospital and at the same time is the head of Cerebrovascular Research Team of the International Clinical Research Center (FNUSA-ICRC). During his work he also deals with pedagogical activities at the Faculty of Medicine of Masaryk University in Brno.
Under his leadership is formed a quality registry of RES-Q (www.qualityregistry.eu), which is already used by over 260 hospitals in 28 countries. He is the initiator and principal investigator of the ESO-EAST project, whose purpose is to improve stroke care in 23 countries of Eastern Europe and Central Asia. He has initiated and he is participating in several research projects for the development of new therapeutic and diagnostic methods of stroke. He also deals with simulation medicine; in 2017 he was appointed as a member of the ESO simulation training committee. Last but not least, he has been involved in raising stroke awareness for over 20 years, and is therefore the guarantor of the HOBIT programme.
He is a member of many national and international organizations focusing on vascular neurology - ESO, ESO-EAST, WSO, CLS JEP, CHS, etc.

Focus

  • vascular neurology,
  • simulation medicine,
  • specialization in stroke simulations,
  • research,
  • diagnosis and timely treatment of stroke,
  • stroke awareness,
  • presidency of national and international congresses.

Awards

Prof. Mikulik was awarded by „Spirit of Excellence“ in May 2017, during the Conference of the European Stroke Organization, for his contribution in a stroke care. He won the award for his long-term and successful activities in the field of organization of stroke care and treatment at national and international level.
